# Homie House video pipeline

Follow `PLAYBOOK.md` for any Homie episode work (concepts, production, publishing). Episode 1 in `episodes/001-the-drip` is the reference for look, pacing and spec format.

- Never use em dashes in captions, Slack posts or any text you write.
- Only publish concepts Max or Aaron approved in #content-updates, and only videos that got an approve reply in their review thread. Never publish straight from the daily run.
- Keep each episode under 150 Revid credits; if it would go over, stop and report in #content-updates.
- Commit `episode.json`, stills, `audio.m4a`, `queue.json` and `log.csv` after each run. Do not commit `out/`.
